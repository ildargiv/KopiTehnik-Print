# Release notes — v1.1.0

- **Photo / Document modes.** Two buttons at the top switch the program. Document mode opens PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, prints them with a page range, copies, paper size and its own printer profile, and edits PDF or recognises text through KopiTehnik Converter. The **Open** button and drag-and-drop accept documents too.
- **New colour tab** (Photo / Colour / Smart frame / Printer): **Levels**, **Curves** and **By colour** (selective colour) with a full-screen editor that shows the edited photo beside it, two user **Presets** (each with its own Save button), and the **Enhance** / **Super** buttons rebuilt on top of the new tools. They reset the photo's previous correction and pick everything from the photo itself; presets are independent of them.
- **Before / After** button next to *Print photo*: drag a line over the photo to compare the original and the edited version.
- **Print counter** in sheets: photos and documents separately, by paper size and by printer, with filters and export.
- **Test sheet** for borderless printing in several languages with large, readable scales.
- Borders (margins) and "screen print simulation" moved to where they belong: the *Sheet* card and the *Printer* tab. Smart frame, update check from GitHub, new program name and animated logo.
- The converter's PDF editor no longer fails on file names with special characters.

Files in the release: `PhotoCropQt.exe`, `PhotoCropAgent.exe`.
